# 2. Send a payment

No code here. You'll set up a real wallet and pay the person next to you.

## Set up your wallet

1. Install the [Varial wallet](https://varialwallet.com) browser extension (formerly Freighter).
2. Create a new wallet.
3. Write your recovery phrase on paper. Never type it into a chat, a form or a repo.
4. Switch the network to **Testnet**.
5. Fund your account with Friendbot. It gives you 10,000 test XLM.

## Pay your neighbour

1. Swap addresses with the person next to you. They start with G.
2. Send them 10 XLM.
3. Wait about five seconds, then check that their balance went up.

## See it on the explorer

Open [stellar.expert/explorer/testnet](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet) and paste your address. Find your payment, then look for the sender, the receiver, the amount and the fee.

Anyone can see this. That's useful when you need proof of payment, and worth remembering when you use real money.

## Stuck?

The extension won't install: use [Stellar Lab](https://lab.stellar.org) to create and fund a testnet account, or pair with your neighbour.

The wallet shows no balance: check that it's on Testnet.

Put your green note up. You've just made a payment on a public network.
