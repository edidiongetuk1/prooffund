## Stellar Community Fund

The [Stellar Community Fund](https://communityfund.stellar.org/awards) (SCF) pays teams to take a project to mainnet. A new round opens every six weeks.

**Build Award:** up to $150K in XLM, paid in four parts:

| Start | MVP | Testnet | Mainnet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 20% | 30% | 40% |

**Three tracks:**

| Track | For |
|---|---|
| Open | A new idea. Experienced teams with a novel use case. The community votes |
| Integration | Building an existing Stellar product into yours |
| RFP | Answering one of the SCF's open requests for proposals, often dev tools |

**How to apply:**

1. Fill in the interest form at [communityfund.stellar.org/awards](https://communityfund.stellar.org/awards).
2. Get invited to a round.
3. A panel reviews your project.
4. Build, and get paid as you hit each milestone.

**Instawards** fund short pieces of work that fit in about 30 days, usually $1K to $5K in XLM. They come through local Stellar Ambassador chapters, so start with Stellar Nigeria.

### Boundless

[Boundless](https://www.boundlessfi.xyz) is where communities run bounties and hackathons, with the prize held in a smart contract. Bounties cover design, writing, video and code, so you don't have to be a developer. Sign up with email and you get a wallet with no seed phrase.

### Drips Stellar Wave

[Stellar Wave](https://www.drips.network/wave/stellar) pays people to fix issues in Stellar projects. Wave 11 runs from 18 to 25 November 2026, with a $75,000 pool.

1. Sign in with GitHub.
2. Complete the ID check. You need a government ID, a selfie and proof of address, so do this before the Wave starts.
3. Find an issue, apply, and wait to be assigned.
4. Open a pull request. When it's merged, you earn points: 100, 150 or 200.
5. When the Wave ends, you get your share of the pool based on your points.

Read [the Drips docs](https://docs.drips.network/wave) before you start.

### GrantFox

[GrantFox](https://contribute.grantfox.xyz) lets you browse open issues on Stellar projects and get rewarded for your contributions. It also has bounties for testing products and reporting what you find, which doesn't need code.

### Stellar Nigeria

Stellar Nigeria is an active [Stellar Ambassador chapter](https://stellar.gitbook.io/ambassador-program/chapters/africa). Join it to hear about events, Instawards and other builders near you. Chapter calls happen on the [Stellar Dev Discord](https://discord.gg/stellardev).

### Bug bounties

Security researchers get paid for finding bugs. Stellar runs a program on [HackerOne](https://hackerone.com/stellar), and [XOXNO Lending](https://immunefi.com/bug-bounty/xoxno/) pays up to $20,000 for critical contract bugs. This is advanced work, so come back to it once you've written a few contracts.

### Hackathons

Stellar hackathons appear regularly on [DoraHacks](https://dorahacks.io) and [Boundless](https://www.boundlessfi.xyz), and past ones have had $10,000 pools. Stellar's own [HackMeridian](https://meridian.stellar.org/hackmeridian) ran in Lisbon in October 2026.
